# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not post credentials, private prompts, or personal data.
- Welcome questions from people with different levels of experience.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that affect technical recommendations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, impersonation, and publishing another person's confidential information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on GitHub.
